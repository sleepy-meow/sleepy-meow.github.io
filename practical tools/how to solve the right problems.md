*A facilitation tool for cross-functional teams designed to create a user-centric conversation*

It's easy to jump the gun and go straight to the "solutions" part, and start adding a feature, and forget to ask the most important thing: are these the right features?


This canvas is worded for ux consultants, but can easily be modified to fit some other use case: https://jeffgothelf.com/blog/leanuxcanvas-v2/

- Helps focus on “why”
- Encourages learning
- Exposes the gaps in the team’s understanding of the problem they’re solving, who they’re solving it for and why they believe their solutions will work
- It’s a first step in the shift of the conversation from outputs to outcomes

The main idea is to focus on one topic at a time:

1. what problem are we trying to solve?
2. How will we know we've solved the problem?
3. What types of users should we focus on first?
4. Why would the users seek out the product? what benefit would the users gain from using it? what behavior change can we observe that tells us they've achieved their goal?
5. what can we make that will solve our problem and meet the needs of the users at the same time?
6. etc
7. etc

When balancing multiple stakeholders it can also be useful to go through 1&2 separately for each, for example: "what problem does the (business, artists, programmers, etc) have we're trying to solve"
