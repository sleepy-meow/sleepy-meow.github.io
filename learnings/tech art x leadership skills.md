
One possible reason why the whole topic of these books resonated is how much they already overlapped with my work as a pipeline technical artist, and the mindset it requires.

- it's a vague job title spanning across multiple domains, so continuous learning is needed
- the technical issues are often surprisingly simple, the real challenge is the detective work needed to understand the problem, and then to get people on the same page on how to solve it, involving both artists and programmers who often don't speak the same language
- the work revolves around enabling others to do their best work, and art pipelines are ultimately one part of the work environment
- The best solution is not necessarily a new tool, but 

> "the minute you encourage someone to use a piece of technology, you are inherently responsible for it" (reid hoffman, masters of scale)
