# why to hire people who think differently
Interviewers tend to prefer candidates who remind them of themselves (similarity bias). 

Using the swiss cheese analogy again, if everyone has the same life experiences and biases, then the holes in the cheese will be in the same places and have blind spots which a more diverse team could easily point out.

# groupthink
Another thing that can happen when a group thinks too much the same way, is groupthink. It occurs when the desire for harmony or conformity in the group results in irrational or dysfunctional decision-making.
