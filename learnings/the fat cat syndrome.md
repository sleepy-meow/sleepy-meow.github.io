# why it happens
- lack of self-restraint & institutional safeguards
- being driven by selfish motives
- unchecked power can literally [reduce the ability to feel empathy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10461512/#:~:text=2%2E3%2E%20Power%20decreases%20empathy%20and%20compassion) (generally doesn't improve over time)

# why it's bad

> _“When the people have to manage dangers from inside the organization, the organization itself becomes less able to face the dangers from outside.”_

- lowers [[psychological safety]]
